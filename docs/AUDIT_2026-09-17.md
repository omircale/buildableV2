# Full audit — 2026-09-17

Scope: every furniture type in the catalog (16), the engine, the editor, dimensions, projects, exports. Done in three layers:

1. **Automated invariant audit** — `src/engine/audit.test.ts` runs every catalog preset *and* every ranged parameter at its minimum, middle and maximum (≈ 330 designs) through the full engine and checks:
   - finite numbers everywhere; every manufactured component is in exactly one cut-list part; reference items (mattress, steel bar) in none;
   - broken geometry (zero-size or overlapping parts) is always reported RED and blocks export — never built silently;
   - the reported overall size is the real footprint;
   - export is blocked **exactly** when a blocking category is RED;
   - assembly steps only reference parts and hardware that exist;
   - English output contains no leftover Hebrew;
   - **every fix offered on a RED check really clears that check when applied.**
2. **Dimension exactness** — `src/ui/measure.test.ts`: for every part of every catalog item, the size drawn on an isolated part equals its cut-list size, and the label texts are generated from those same numbers (no rounding beyond 0.1 mm).
3. **Browser run** — all 16 types opened; every step; every view mode; one component per role selected and isolated; advanced panel; English mode. No console errors.

Result: **2,023 tests pass**; build clean.

## Findings and fixes

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Isolating a part and switching to *Dimensions* still showed the whole unit's dimensions. There was no true isolate. | High (user report) | **Fixed** — "Isolate this part" in the part panel shows only that part and frames the camera on it; *Dimensions* then draws that part's own width/height/depth (thickness named as such) and its cut-list size (`P2 · cut size 76 × 29 × 1.8 cm`). |
| 2 | Dimension labels were rounded to the millimetre (34.35 cm showed as 34.4). | Medium | **Fixed** — one exact formatter (`formatCm`) everywhere: 3D labels, part panel, section summaries, review, projects, search results. |
| 3 | No place to see all projects; starting a new design replaced the current one (only Undo could bring it back). | High (user report) | **Fixed** — local-first **My projects** (`#/projects`, header link, recent projects on home, ⌘K). Every design autosaves; open / rename / duplicate / delete; switching clears Undo so one project's history never leaks into another. |
| 4 | Drilling notes (Ø35 hinge cups) and 45° rafter cuts were computed but never reached the factory: missing from CSV, cut list, order text and PDF. | High | **Fixed** — `Machining` column in CSV, a highlighted row in the cut list, a section in the copied order text, a column in the PDF. |
| 5 | Supplier "part too large" fixes were offered even when they could not help (a 270 cm tall unit was offered "width 118 cm"). | Medium | **Fixed** — height/width-to-maximum fixes added; every supplier fix is re-quoted and offered only if all parts become orderable. |
| 6 | Pull-up station: foot boards stuck out beyond the reported overall width (≈ 9 cm each side). | Medium | **Fixed** — geometry shifted so the footprint starts at 0; overall width is the real footprint. |
| 7 | Bench/table with a middle panel: assembly steps referenced part ids that no longer existed. | Low | **Fixed**. |
| 8 | Low table/nightstand with no room for a lower shelf was offered "shelf at 10 cm", which still failed. | Low | **Fixed** — that fix is offered only when it fits; "no lower shelf" always offered. |
| 9 | Safety sources for beds and chairs stayed Hebrew in English mode. | Low | **Fixed** — template check sources are localized. |
| 10 | Choosing a furniture type from ⌘K search only navigated to the editor without starting that furniture. | Medium | **Fixed** — it starts a new project of that type. |
| 11 | Editor at ~1024 px: header and bottom toolbar pushed the page sideways (horizontal scroll). | Medium | **Fixed** — toolbar scrolls inside itself; projects link hidden in the cramped editor header (still in ⌘K and on home). |
| 12 | Projects card: RTL reordered "W × H × D · ₪price". | Low | **Fixed** — numbers in LTR spans. |
| 13 | Wording "No back panel (not recommended)" contradicted the neutral-wording rule. | Low | **Fixed** — "less sideways stiffness — see stability". |
| 14 | Review screen showed shelf wording for other furniture ("shelves may sag", "anchor to the wall"). | Medium | **Fixed** earlier the same day — failing rule/member named; each template's own safety notes listed. |

## Confirmed as designed (not bugs)

- A double bed with a 180×200 mattress on 18 mm slats is RED: two 110 kg sleepers over an 88 cm half-span exceed L/150. The fix list offers the changes that clear it.
- Pull-up station is always blocked pending a structural engineer.
- Only birch 18 mm has strength data; other boards give GREY on loaded members.

## Not verifiable here

- Live 3D label rendering was checked visually on a shelf; for the rest, the label texts are verified by unit tests because the browser pane was hidden (a hidden pane does not render WebGL frames).
- Physical strength, joints, stability and child-safety compliance still need physical tests — the product says so on every design.
